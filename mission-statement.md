# Mission Statement

## Build, Don't Bleed

My objective is to turn money, creativity, and technical knowledge into sustainable independence.

I will keep essential living costs separate from trading capital and project budgets. I will create before purchasing, reward completed work rather than impulse, preserve realized profits, and review spending regularly.

I will measure entrepreneurial progress by my ability to turn limited resources into finished work, retained capital, and repeatable value—not by how much money I can risk.

**My mission is not to stop taking chances. It is to stop making my survival depend on them.**
